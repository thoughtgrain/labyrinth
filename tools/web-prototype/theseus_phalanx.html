Pads Tap, or press 1 to 8. Each is a pitch above the held key; Theseus picks the drum from the pitch.

The sequencer, the drum voice and every line on the screen come from meikyu_core.c, theseus.c and theseus_screen.c, unchanged. The parts the OP-1 does itself are stand-ins here: six voices round robin, an instant-attack, long-release envelope and a soft clip on the output. Sound starts with Play.
