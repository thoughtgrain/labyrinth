synth

shows the loaded engine. press it again to swap toro and theseus. turn the encoders to play the sound; tap blue, green or white for page 2.

sequencer

shows meikyu. hold a key to play the pattern from it; shift + key or play keeps it going. on this screen the encoders are corr, len, dens, rng; shift for rate, gate, scl, hold; tap orange for the mod page.

keys

z–m and q–i play the two octaves; hold shift for shift. turn the sequencer off (shift + sequencer) to play notes straight into the engine.
