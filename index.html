labyrinth

whatthree plugins for the op-1: a sequencer that rewrites itself, a synth that folds, a drum voice that plays a kit from one oscillator pair.
runs onop-1 with hundekraft 3.0.3 (kennel)
controls
licensegpl-3.0-or-later, open source

meikyu

sequencer

a 16-step shift register that rewrites itself as it plays. it checks which engine is loaded: melodies on toro, eight drum pads on theseus.

encoders: turn / shift + turn
corrchance a step is rewritten / rate
len2 to 16 steps / gate
denshow many steps play / scl
rngpitch range / hold

toro

synth

two oscillators, a wavefolder and a resonant low-pass. aphx gives every key its own small drift, then pulls the whole thing apart.

encoders: turn / press for page 2
sprdosc 2 at 1.0 to 1.5x / shap
fold1 to 8x folder / nois
cutlow-pass / driv 0-50 %, aphx 50-100 %
resresonance

theseus

drum voice

dfam-style percussion: the note picks the drum, from kick to metal. an lfo steps once per hit, so it locks to the pattern.

encoders: turn / press for page 2
dcy30 ms to 1.5 s / nois
pnchpitch drop / lfo depth
cutfilter / rate 32 to 2 hits
fmskins to clangs

play it in a browser

all three plugins' own c code, compiled to webassembly, in a very simple instrument: shift, synth and sequencer keys, four encoders, a keyboard. synth swaps toro and theseus; sequencer shows meikyu; hold a key to play the pattern.

open the instrument

install

  1. get the matching hundekraft 3.0.3 source and sdk, and a blackfin gcc.
  2. copy the three folders into ext/kennel/synth_sdk/plugins/ and pack each one:
python3 tools/kennel/pack.py synth_sdk/plugins/meikyu  -o build/meikyu.knl
python3 tools/kennel/pack.py synth_sdk/plugins/toro    -o build/toro.knl
python3 tools/kennel/pack.py synth_sdk/plugins/theseus -o build/theseus.knl
  1. install the .knl files in the workshop: your plugins, update op-1.
